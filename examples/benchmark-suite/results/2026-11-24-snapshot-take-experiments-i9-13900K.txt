# Snapshot-take experiments, recorded 2026-11-24 (UTC) on the shared dev box

Raw output of the runs behind docs/design-notes/snapshot-take-cost.md, produced by
scripts/snapshot-profile.sh (columns are milliseconds, medians over the runs
shown; the daemon's own snapshot_take_phase_duration_ms steps plus the HTTP time).
Two daemon builds were used: the repository build, and for E6 a temporary build
that also sent Firecracker 1.17.0's sync_snapshot_files=false (not in the
repository). The state directory is on the ext4 root disk unless a section says
otherwise; the default warm pool was off.

Part 1 holds E1, E1b, E2, E5 and E7. Its first attempt at the size and dirty
sweeps ran all full-write cells before all diff cells, so disk drift favoured one
side; those cells were rerun alternated in part 2 and the first attempt is not
reproduced here.
